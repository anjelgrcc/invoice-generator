# Invoice Generator

A simple, free invoice maker that runs entirely in your browser. No account, no server, no install.

**Use it here:** https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/invoice-generator/

## What it does

- Enter your business details once and reuse them on every invoice
- Save clients, each with their own list of projects
- Add line items by project, with an optional description, quantity and rate
- Choose a currency and tax percentage; subtotal, tax and total are calculated for you
- Number invoices automatically (INV-0001, INV-0002, ...). Change the number on any invoice and the next ones continue from it
- Mark invoices as paid or unpaid
- Dashboard with totals for outstanding, overdue and paid invoices, plus filters and a per-client summary
- Clean print layout. Use **Print or save PDF** and choose "Save as PDF" in the print dialog

## Your data stays with you

Everything is saved in your own browser (localStorage). Nothing is uploaded anywhere, and other people who use the app cannot see your invoices. The flip side: clearing your browser data erases your invoices, and they do not sync between devices.
